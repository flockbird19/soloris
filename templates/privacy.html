🔒 Privacy & Security

Your data belongs to you. Here is exactly how we handle it.

1. What Data We Collect

When you upload a Death Certificate, we extract basic details (Name, Date of Death, Registration Number) to auto-fill your claim forms.

When you connect Gmail, we only scan emails from trusted financial domains (like banks and insurance companies) to discover accounts.

2. How Your Data is Protected

Your session is completely isolated. We use secure browser sessions to ensure your data is never mixed with anyone else's.

3. When Data is Deleted

We do not store your data long-term. All data extracted during this session is instantly wiped from our memory the moment you close your browser or your session expires.
